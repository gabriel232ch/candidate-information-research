# Research bibliography

The public research outputs in this archive are the evidence syntheses used by the timeline and method notes:

- [Smaller, high-impact companies: public synthesis](../research/01_smaller_high_impact_companies/public_synthesis.md). A purposively selected comparison of mechanisms, enabling conditions, and counterexamples. Its [source and claim map](../research/01_smaller_high_impact_companies/sources.md) distinguishes company statements, regulatory filings, a historical secondary report, and the project's own method record.
- [Five employer-brand cases: cross-case synthesis](../research/02_employer_brand_case_studies/cross_case_synthesis.md). A working interpretation of five independently documented cases. Its [source register](../research/02_employer_brand_case_studies/sources.md) and [case index](../research/02_employer_brand_case_studies/README.md) preserve the case-level evidence and limits.
- [Evaluation design](../methods/evaluation_design.md). A bounded account of the early qualitative exploration and the later single-matrix observations. The underlying private answer data are intentionally absent from this public bibliography.

No external academic paper is used here to establish the project's internal chronology, evaluation counts, causal effects, or candidate outcomes. Public company pages and other web evidence are indexed in [external links](external_links.md); their company-specific claims retain the narrower citations in the research sections.
