# From Golden Samples to a reusable content process

## The recurring decision

A candidate question is more specific than a request for an attractive employer story. It may ask whether a role fits a research interest, what kind of work happens after joining, or what trade-offs accompany a career move. The early writing work required editorial choices about which question to answer, which evidence could support it, and where uncertainty should remain visible.

Three satisfactory article transformations became Golden Samples for those decisions. They were used to articulate a reusable internal writing process rather than to publish a template or copy the examples. At a public level, its sequence was **candidate query → direct answer → relevant employee evidence → fuller story and context → review**. The review checked evidence alignment, natural language, and whether the answer overstated what an individual account could establish. Neither private examples nor internal instructions are reproduced here.

The first article took approximately **2–3 hours** in the reported working context; the next two took approximately **20–30 minutes each**. These are approximate firsthand estimates, not independently timed measurements. After the process stabilized, six candidate-query-oriented story drafts were prepared from interview material. Draft completion does not establish public release or an effect on AI answers or candidate decisions.

## What transferred

The reusable part was a set of editorial judgments: keep the candidate's actual question in view; distinguish a direct answer from supporting narrative; use evidence only for claims it can bear; preserve a person's perspective and the limits of that perspective; and make the final text subject to human review. An interview account is situated evidence. It cannot by itself represent every role, team, or employee.

The later [evaluation design](evaluation_design.md) checked answer behavior after this content work was prepared. It was a separate measurement exercise, not a proof that the drafts changed that behavior. The broader [method evolution](../METHOD_EVOLUTION.md) also records the independent research-system loop.
