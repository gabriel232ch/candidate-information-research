# Method evolution

Two parts of this inquiry became more explicit as repeated work exposed decisions that were easy to leave implicit. These are process histories, not performance benchmarks or claims that a tool replaced editorial or research judgment.

## From examples to a reusable content process

The content work began with careful human judgment over whether an answer met a candidate's question and whether its evidence and language were trustworthy. Three satisfactory transformations served as Golden Samples. The team then expressed recurring decisions as a reusable internal process: identify the candidate query, answer it directly, choose support that actually bears on the answer, retain relevant trade-offs, and review the result. [Method note](methods/golden_samples_to_reusable_system.md)

In that same working context, the first article was estimated at approximately **2–3 hours** and the next two at approximately **20–30 minutes each**. These are firsthand process estimates without independent time tracking. They describe that sequence, not a general speed claim. Six subsequent candidate-query-oriented story drafts were prepared; the record does not establish publication or candidate response.

## From manual cases to a reusable research protocol

The first employer-brand cases required sequential source collection, claim checking, case boundaries, and synthesis by hand. Repetition made it useful to specify source rules, a consistent case structure, decision gates, and verification steps that could be applied across cases. The resulting research harness supported parallel case work while keeping case-specific evidence and limitations visible. [Method note](methods/manual_cases_to_research_harness.md)

The project estimate was approximately **one day** for the first case, **half a day each** for the second and third, and **most of a working day** to design and build the harness. Two later cases ran in parallel within roughly the same **one-hour-plus wall-clock window**. These estimates have no independent elapsed-time log and mix different kinds of work. They do not measure a controlled efficiency gain.

The shared move in both loops was to make judgment inspectable and repeatable: **manual editorial judgment → explicit content process** and **manual case research → reusable parallel research protocol**. Human review remained necessary for factual support, context, privacy, and whether the answer actually helped a candidate decide.
