# Sources and claim map

All company pages and official repositories below were checked live on **2026-10-02**, unless a different publication date is stated. First-party sources establish what the organization publishes about itself; they are not independent verification of employee experience, typical practice, commercial performance, or causal effects. The source IDs are used in `public_synthesis.md` and `README.md`.

## Project method record

### P1. Project method record

The project-reported 28-company longlist, 12 structured screens, six deep dives, two counterexamples, and five working archetypes were checked against the study's local selection criteria, candidate longlist, case-screening matrix, and final analysis files. These counts and selections describe the research process; they are not external-company facts or a population estimate. The complete longlist and internal screening files are not reproduced in this archive.
