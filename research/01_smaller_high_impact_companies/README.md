# Smaller organizations, high impact

This archive section summarizes an independent, purposively selected comparison of organizations whose impact appeared notable relative to their scale. The research question was: **which specific mechanisms, when present, can help a relatively small organization produce meaningful output and attract mission-critical talent?** The project does not treat smallness itself as an advantage. [P1](sources.md#p1-project-method-record)

## Study scope

The project reports a 28-company longlist, 12 structured screens, six deep dives, and two counterexamples. The deep dives were Linear, 37signals, XTX Markets, Pentagram, Midjourney, and DeepSeek. Allbirds and IDEO were selected as counterexamples. This is the project's purposive sample, not a representative sample of smaller companies. [P1](sources.md#p1-project-method-record)

## Evidence boundary

The archetypes are interpretive patterns in this selected set. They are not causal findings, validated employment-outcome measures, or universal management recipes. Company pages document what organizations choose to say publicly; they do not independently establish employee experience, candidate use, or hiring and retention outcomes. Source pages were checked live on 2026-10-02 where noted. The IDEO contraction example is based on a dated 2023 secondary report and is not a claim about IDEO's current status.
