# Employer-brand case studies: candidate information

This section asks how publicly inspectable employer information can help a candidate investigate a role, the work, and the trade-offs of joining. Five cases were selected as a comparative set: Optiver, Two Sigma, Citadel Securities, Canva, and GitLab. Each case is read on its own terms before the [cross-case synthesis](cross_case_synthesis.md). The selection is purposive and cannot show how common these practices are among employers.

## Read the cases

- [Optiver](01_optiver.md): early-career role choice and staged exposure to trading work.
- [Two Sigma](02_two_sigma.md): inspectable technical and academic activity beyond recruiting copy.
- [Citadel Securities](03_citadel_securities.md): role-specific responsibility and selective early-career experiences.
- [Canva](04_canva.md): information for several points in the application and career decision.
- [GitLab](05_gitlab.md): an open operating handbook, with maintenance and navigation demands.
- [Cross-case synthesis](cross_case_synthesis.md): provisional mechanisms, narrowed ideas, unsupported propositions, and boundary conditions.
- [Sources](sources.md): official pages used in this section and their evidentiary limits.

## Reading boundary

The pages cited here are published by the companies themselves and were checked on 2026-10-02. They show what an interested reader could inspect on that date and what each company says about itself. They do not independently establish everyday practice, how many candidates find or use the material, whether a selected example is typical, or hiring and retention outcomes. The synthesis is this research project's working interpretation, not a framework claimed by the companies.
