# Cross-case synthesis: candidate decision evidence

This is a provisional interpretation of five purposively selected, evidence-rich public cases, not a representative survey or a causal theory. The companies do not claim a shared framework. Each case answers a different candidate question: [Optiver](01_optiver.md) helps distinguish unfamiliar early-career paths; [Two Sigma](02_two_sigma.md) exposes technical and academic artifacts; [Citadel Securities](03_citadel_securities.md) describes responsibility and selective routes; [Canva](04_canva.md) supplies inputs across application stages; [GitLab](05_gitlab.md) opens operating documents. Company-published pages show what is publicly inspectable and what the company asserts. They cannot, alone, establish how work is experienced or how hiring performs. [Sources and evidence limits](sources.md)

## Working model

**Candidate Friction → Evidence Need → Organisational Evidence → Evidence Interface → Answer Legibility → Reality / Governance Check**

This chain is a **working model** for asking whether employer information can support a concrete career decision. Candidate friction is the uncertainty or trade-off a person faces. Evidence need identifies what would reduce that uncertainty. Organisational evidence is the relevant work, process, artifact, or constraint an employer can show. The evidence interface is how a candidate reaches and interprets it. Answer legibility asks whether the evidence can support a direct, qualified answer. The final check asks whether the answer is current, representative enough for its scope, and consistent with the role's reality. The chain is an analytical aid for these cases; it has not been tested as an outcome model.

## Stable mechanisms in this selected set

**Start from a decision, then choose evidence.** Optiver's role explainer distinguishes quantitative trading from research, while its internship page makes work constraints more concrete. GitLab's candidate page starts with hiring stages and role-conditional assessments. Both show why a candidate question is more precise than a generic request for “more employer content.” [Optiver role explainer](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/stories/breaking-down-the-trading-industry-which-role-is-right-for-you/) · [Optiver research internship](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/students/internships/research/) · [GitLab candidate page](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/hiring/candidate-faq/)

**Use evidence from where the work is visible.** Two Sigma's open source and academic pages allow inspection beyond a careers narrative. GitLab's public Handbook describes intended operating processes. These sources have different strengths: a software artifact is inspectable output, while a handbook is a published account of process. Neither proves the full internal experience, but both can support questions that recruiting copy cannot answer alone. [Two Sigma Open Source](https://www.twosigma.com/open-source/) · [Two Sigma Academic Partnerships](https://www.twosigma.com/academic-partnerships/) · [GitLab Handbook Usage](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/about/handbook-usage/)

**Connect evidence to a specific role and moment.** Canva's process guide distinguishes general stages from engineering variations, and its team pages and career tools address other parts of the decision. Citadel Securities separates quantitative research from engineering and offers a role-specific FAQ. An interface has to help candidates make those connections without implying that a broad claim applies identically to every vacancy. [Canva How We Hire](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/how-we-hire/) · [Canva Engineering](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/teams/engineering/) · [Citadel Securities role pages](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/quantitative-research/) · [Citadel Securities FAQ](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/career-perspectives/candidate-faqs-quantitative-research/)

**Attach scope, trade-offs, and maintenance to the answer.** Pages about program access, interview steps, responsibility, growth, and ways of working are useful because they invite concrete follow-up. They must also identify where eligibility, role, region, cohort, and date change the answer. GitLab's dated Handbook Direction page is a reminder that an open document still needs maintenance and a current check before its roadmap is treated as present practice. [Optiver FutureFocus](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/students/programs/futurefocus/) · [Citadel Securities Programs and Events](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/programs-and-events/) · [Canva How We Hire](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/how-we-hire/) · [GitLab Handbook Direction](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/about/direction/)

## Narrowed and context-sensitive mechanisms

**Talent Translation** helps when a candidate has transferable skills but lacks a map into an unfamiliar field or role. Optiver's distinction among trading and research roles and its student pathways make this plausible for early-career STEM audiences. It is narrower for candidates who already know the domain, and a translation claim is weak if it cannot be anchored in actual tasks and eligibility. A program is one possible interface, not a required format. [Optiver role explainer](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/stories/breaking-down-the-trading-industry-which-role-is-right-for-you/) · [Optiver Students](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/students/)

**Public technical artifacts** can be strong for candidates able to evaluate them and for roles to which they are relevant. Two Sigma's open source material cannot stand for every job or for nonpublic research. **Selective experiences** can show a possibility and give some participants a taste of a field; Citadel Securities' invitation-only programs have access and selection boundaries. **Extensive documentation** can expose process, as at GitLab, while imposing navigation and freshness costs. **Broad career libraries** can cover many questions, as at Canva, but need role-level routing and consistency. [Two Sigma Open Source](https://www.twosigma.com/open-source/) · [Citadel Securities Programs and Events](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/programs-and-events/) · [GitLab Handbook Usage](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/about/handbook-usage/) · [Canva How We Hire](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/how-we-hire/)

## Rejected or unsupported propositions

- **A polished source proves lived reality.** These are mostly first-party materials. A published capability may exist while some teams, roles, or candidates encounter it differently; one visible path cannot describe the distribution of experience. [Sources and evidence limits](sources.md)
- **A selected success story is a normal career trajectory.** Citadel Securities' pages describe accelerated responsibility and selected examples, but the pages do not give a full-time denominator or separate selection from development. The case cannot establish typical speed of advancement or fairness. [Citadel Securities Quantitative Research](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/quantitative-research/) · [Citadel Securities Engineering](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/engineering/)
- **More pages automatically produce clarity or better outcomes.** Canva and GitLab show the need for navigation, currency, and role-specific confirmation. This study has no candidate task test or outcome measure. [Canva How We Hire](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/how-we-hire/) · [GitLab candidate page](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/hiring/candidate-faq/)
- **A format or public artifact automatically improves machine-generated answers.** The cases establish available source material, not how any model retrieves, cites, or uses it. No retrieval or visibility result follows from these cases.
- **One template transfers across employers.** The five contexts differ in audience, access, disclosure constraints, work type, and resources. A five-day student program, open source portfolio, selective event, career library, and public handbook solve different information problems. [Optiver FutureFocus](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/students/programs/futurefocus/) · [Two Sigma Open Source](https://www.twosigma.com/open-source/) · [Citadel Securities Programs and Events](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/programs-and-events/) · [Canva Career Tools](https://www.lifeatcanva.com/en/career-tools/) · [GitLab Handbook Usage](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/about/handbook-usage/)

## Boundary conditions and next questions

The useful unit of comparison is **candidate question + relevant evidence + access path + claim limit**. Role and geography affect what applies. Selective programs can give rich evidence to a narrow group. Proprietary work may limit what can be shown publicly. Large public corpora require search and upkeep. Every source needs a date and a route for checking role-level reality. Citadel Securities and Citadel must remain separate entities when sources are attributed. [Citadel Securities careers](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/) · [GitLab Handbook Direction](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/about/direction/)

The next empirical questions would require candidate navigation studies, role-level confirmation, and outcome measures: can a candidate find the relevant evidence, understand its scope, make a better-informed choice, and later recognize the described work? These cases generate those questions. They do not answer them.
