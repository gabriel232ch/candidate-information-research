# Sources and claim boundaries

All company links below are first-party public pages checked live on 2026-10-02. They support statements about published information and company descriptions on that date. They are not independent verification of internal practice, applicant use, representativeness, fairness, or employment outcomes. The five-case synthesis is the research project's interpretation. No private source material is reproduced here.
